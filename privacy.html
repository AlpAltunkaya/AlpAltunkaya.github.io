Privacy policy

Alp de Gmail is a personal app with one user, Alp Altunkaya.

  • It reads, labels and drafts mail only in Alp's own Gmail account, after he signs in.
  • Login tokens are stored only on Alp's own computer.
  • No data is sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • Use of data received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • To remove access, revoke "Alp de Gmail" in the Google account's third-party connections.

Contact: open an issue at github.com/AlpAltunkaya/alpaltunkaya.github.io/issues.
